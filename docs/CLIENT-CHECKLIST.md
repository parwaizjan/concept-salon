# Client checklist — what we need from a new salon

This is a **client-facing** document. It is written for a salon owner, not for a
developer. Use it during the pitch and to collect everything needed to build and
launch their site. It is safe to copy the questions or the ready-to-send WhatsApp
message at the bottom straight to the owner.

Do not add developer jargon, internal architecture, or credentials to this file.

## What the salon gets

- A fast, mobile-friendly website that shows every service and price.
- Clients browse services, see an estimate, and send an appointment request to the
  salon **on WhatsApp** — no apps, no payment gateway, no commissions.
- A photo gallery of the salon's best work.
- The owner can update **prices and availability** themselves, for free, from a
  simple Google Sheet.
- Website hosting costs the salon **nothing per month**.
- When live, the site can appear on Google and be shared on Facebook and WhatsApp
  with a proper preview.
- Sinhala and Tamil can be added on request (English is included).

## 1. Business basics

- [ ] Salon / brand name (exactly as it should appear).
- [ ] A short tagline or one-line description.
- [ ] A short "our story" paragraph (a few sentences; we can polish it).
- [ ] Logo file, if they have one (PNG or SVG on a plain or transparent background).
- [ ] Brand colours — or tell us to choose for them.
- [ ] Any font or style they must match (optional).
- [ ] Photos for the top of the home page and the gallery.

## 2. Contact details

- [ ] **WhatsApp number that should receive appointment requests** (with country
      code).
- [ ] Phone number shown on the site.
- [ ] Email (optional — for display only; requests go to WhatsApp).
- [ ] Facebook / Instagram / TikTok links (optional).
- [ ] Who replies to requests, and roughly when they are available.

## 3. Location and opening hours

- [ ] Full address as it should be written.
- [ ] Google Maps location (a shared pin/link is perfect).
- [ ] Opening hours for each day, including closed days.
- [ ] Special or holiday hours, if any (optional).
- [ ] Parking / landmark directions, if useful (optional).

## 4. How they take appointments

- [ ] Can clients walk in, or is booking recommended/preferred?
- [ ] Preferred time windows you would like clients to choose from (e.g. 9am–1pm,
      2pm–7pm).
- [ ] How much notice you usually need (e.g. "same day is fine", "book a day ahead").
- [ ] Do certain services need a consultation or a deposit? (We will show a note; the
      owner confirms details in chat — no payments on the site.)
- [ ] Any services that need a phone call instead of WhatsApp (optional).

## 5. Services and prices

For every service, we need:

- [ ] Category (e.g. Hair, Colour, Nails, Skin & Facials, Bridal, Waxing).
- [ ] Service name.
- [ ] Price in LKR (or "from" a starting price for packages).
- [ ] How long it usually takes (optional but helpful).
- [ ] One short sentence describing it (optional but recommended).
- [ ] A photo (optional to start — we can launch with placeholders and add photos
      later).
- [ ] Sinhala and/or Tamil name, if they have it (optional; we can arrange
      translation).
- [ ] Which services are their specialties or should be featured.

A simple table is enough — the owner can fill it in a note or a spreadsheet:

| Category | Service          | Price (LKR) | Duration | Description                    | Photo? |
| -------- | ---------------- | ----------- | -------- | ------------------------------ | ------ |
| Nails    | Gel Manicure     | 4500        | 60 min   | Long-lasting gel polish finish | yes    |
| Hair     | Haircut & Finish | 3500        | 60 min   | Wash, cut and blow-dry finish  | no     |

We will handle service IDs, image sizing and formatting — the owner just supplies
the above.

## 6. Photos

- [ ] A wide photo for the top of the home page.
- [ ] Gallery photos: hair styles, colour work, nails, facials, the salon interior
      (as many as possible — natural light, no screenshots or watermarks).
- [ ] Salon front / reception.
- [ ] The owner or team (optional).
- [ ] Any logos or signage.

Tips to share with the owner:

- Send the **original** photos, not forwards/screenshots. WhatsApp compresses
  images — for important photos, send as a **document** or share a Google
  Drive / Google Photos link.
- Natural daylight looks best. A clean background beats a busy one.
- It is fine to start with a few photos and add more later.

## 7. Price and availability updates (Google Sheet)

- [ ] A Gmail address the salon controls (ideally a dedicated salon account, not a
      personal one) so we can set up their price sheet.
- [ ] The person who will update prices and mark services unavailable.
- [ ] We set up the Sheet, show them how to use it, and keep a backup of the current
      prices on our side.

## 8. Domain and languages

- [ ] Preferred web address. A free option is `<salon-name>.zuvo.lk`. The owner can
      also use their own domain if they have one (or want to buy one).
- [ ] Languages needed: English is included. Sinhala and/or Tamil can be added.
- [ ] Which language should be the default?

## 9. Sign-off

- [ ] The person who approves the final site.
- [ ] Any existing brand guidelines or marketing material to match.
- [ ] A deadline or event the site should be ready for (optional).

## What we do NOT need

- No payment gateway or online payments.
- No booking/calendar account.
- No bank details or payment credentials.
- No hosting or server accounts — hosting is included and free.
- No technical skills from the owner.
- No professional writing or professional photos to get started.

## What happens next

1. You send us the items above (in any order).
2. We build the site and show you a preview link.
3. You review and tell us what to change.
4. We connect the salon's WhatsApp number and Google Sheet.
5. The site goes live on the chosen web address.
6. We show the owner how to update prices and mark services unavailable.

## Ready-to-send WhatsApp message

Copy and send this to the salon owner:

```text
Hi! To build your salon website, please send me the following when you can.

1. Salon name, a short description, and your logo (if you have one).
2. The WhatsApp number where you want to receive appointment requests, plus a phone number.
3. Your full address, a Google Maps location, and your opening hours (with closed days).
4. Your services and prices: service name, price in LKR, how long it takes, a short description, and a photo if you have one. Please group them by category (hair, colour, nails, facials, bridal, waxing, etc.).
5. Which services are your specialties or should be featured.
6. A few good photos for the website and gallery (send originals, or a Google Drive link).
7. A Gmail address you control so I can set up a simple price sheet you can update yourself.
8. Which languages you want: English, Sinhala, Tamil. English is included by default.
9. Do you want a free web address like yoursalon.zuvo.lk, or your own domain?

You do not need online payments, hosting, or any technical setup. I will handle that.
Send whatever you have first, and we can fill in the rest later.
```
