title: Our story
---

Amara Studio is a small, independent hair and beauty studio in Colombo 07. We
opened with one idea: good hair and skin care should feel calm, personal and
unhurried.

Our stylists and therapists are trained in cutting, colour, nails and skin, and we
take the time to understand what you actually want before we start. No rushing, no
upselling — just work we are proud to put our name to.

Whether it is a quick trim, a full colour, a set of nails or a bridal package for
your big day, you will get the same care and attention every time. We keep our
prices clear and honest, and you can book in a minute on WhatsApp.
